### About the Group

The **Sabre Kais Group** develops theory and algorithms at the interface of
quantum information, quantum computation, and chemistry. We study entanglement
and coherence in complex chemical and biological systems, design quantum
algorithms for molecular simulation, and advance quantum machine learning for
scientific datasets.

Professor Kais directs the [Center for Quantum Technologies](https://www.purdue.edu/cqt/)
and is a Distinguished Professor of Chemistry at Purdue University, with
appointments in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

### Research Highlights

- Quantum coherence and entanglement in chemical and biological systems
- Adiabatic and gate-model quantum algorithms for quantum chemistry
- Quantum machine learning and quantum state tomography
- Quantum simulation of open systems, spin liquids, and radical-pair dynamics
